# Dirección visual y prompts

Método: herramienta integrada ImageGen, dos imágenes originales; conversión y reducción a WebP con Pillow. Los archivos utilizados por la app son `ocean.webp` e `island.webp`.

## Mar

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless-feeling wide ocean background for a romantic interactive island adventure website. Primary request: cinematic straight-down aerial view of open tropical ocean water, realistic deep midnight teal blue ocean with turquoise undercurrents, delicate organic wavelets, subtle warm golden hour glints toward upper right, atmospheric soft shadow in lower left, tranquil luxurious feeling, detailed natural water texture. Composition: wide landscape 1536x1024, water fills entire image edge to edge, relatively even low contrast suitable behind interface. No land, no islands, no ships, no people, no text, no logos, no border, no watermark. Photographic realism, avoid graphic illustrated waves and repetitive geometric patterns.

## Isla

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent top-down island game sprite, final production art for a romantic tropical archipelago web game. A single small tropical island seen directly from above, organic irregular oval coastline, realistic pale champagne sand beach around entire perimeter, lush dense varied forest with distinguishable palm crowns, a few weathered mossy rocky hills in the center, subtle tiny turquoise shallows hugging the beach only. Realistic high detail miniature aerial photography / physically rendered diorama with natural vegetation and believable soft golden afternoon light from upper left. Land centered, fully visible with generous transparent margins on every side. Transparent background outside the island, no rectangular ocean background, no horizon, no sky, no text, no icons, no labels, no people, no buildings, no border. Shape wider than tall, not round symmetrical, gentle asymmetry. The silhouette and terrain must remain legible at 200 pixels wide. Avoid cartoon vector art, avoid low poly mountains, avoid emojis.
