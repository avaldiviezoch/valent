# Dirección visual y prompts

Método: herramienta integrada ImageGen, siete imágenes originales; conversión y reducción a WebP con Pillow. Los archivos utilizados por la app son `ocean.webp`, `island.webp` y cinco paisajes nuevos descritos a continuación.

## Mar

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless-feeling wide ocean background for a romantic interactive island adventure website. Primary request: cinematic straight-down aerial view of open tropical ocean water, realistic deep midnight teal blue ocean with turquoise undercurrents, delicate organic wavelets, subtle warm golden hour glints toward upper right, atmospheric soft shadow in lower left, tranquil luxurious feeling, detailed natural water texture. Composition: wide landscape 1536x1024, water fills entire image edge to edge, relatively even low contrast suitable behind interface. No land, no islands, no ships, no people, no text, no logos, no border, no watermark. Photographic realism, avoid graphic illustrated waves and repetitive geometric patterns.

## Isla

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent top-down island game sprite, final production art for a romantic tropical archipelago web game. A single small tropical island seen directly from above, organic irregular oval coastline, realistic pale champagne sand beach around entire perimeter, lush dense varied forest with distinguishable palm crowns, a few weathered mossy rocky hills in the center, subtle tiny turquoise shallows hugging the beach only. Realistic high detail miniature aerial photography / physically rendered diorama with natural vegetation and believable soft golden afternoon light from upper left. Land centered, fully visible with generous transparent margins on every side. Transparent background outside the island, no rectangular ocean background, no horizon, no sky, no text, no icons, no labels, no people, no buildings, no border. Shape wider than tall, not round symmetrical, gentle asymmetry. The silhouette and terrain must remain legible at 200 pixels wide. Avoid cartoon vector art, avoid low poly mountains, avoid emojis.


## Cinco paisajes distintos para el archipiélago

Cada recurso se generó de forma independiente con ImageGen y fondo transparente. Se conserva la isla tropical original para El encuentro. No se reutiliza su dibujo en los otros destinos.

### Instrucciones comunes

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: production transparent island sprite for a romantic maritime interactive map. True top-down aerial drone view, entire island fully visible and centered, not perspective/isometric, natural photographic detail readable at 250 pixels wide. Warm afternoon sunlight from upper left, cohesive realistic oceanic landscape, natural coastal erosion, fine visible sand grains and tree crowns. Wide landscape composition, island footprint occupies about 82% of image width and 65% height with generous transparent margins. Outside the land and a very narrow rim of shallow turquoise water and foam, use REAL transparent alpha, no solid backdrop, no rectangle of sea, no horizon or sky. No text, no labels, no icons, no ships, no people, no buildings, no hearts, no fantasy glowing props, no cartoon, no low-poly geometry.

### island-crescent.webp

Prompt completo: instrucciones comunes anteriores más el siguiente texto.

Subject: a distinct slender C-shaped tropical crescent island, pale ivory sandy arms curling around an open turquoise sheltered bay that opens to the right. Dense low palm grove along the outer spine, shallow reef edge, no central rocky mountain. The crescent silhouette must be unmistakable, asymmetrical tips one longer than the other. Interior bay water softly fades into transparency where it meets open sea.

### island-twins.webp

Prompt completo: instrucciones comunes anteriores más el siguiente texto.

Subject: two clearly distinct small tropical islets connected diagonally by a narrow winding white sandbar, a natural double island. Upper left islet rocky and forested, lower right islet rounder and covered in palm crowns, broad clean sandy edges and turquoise tidal shallows around the thin curving bridge. The silhouette must read as two separate land masses with a slender sandy neck. No tall central mountain.

### island-volcanic.webp

Prompt completo: instrucciones comunes anteriores más el siguiente texto.

Subject: a rugged compact near-triangular volcanic ocean island, unmistakable dark charcoal basalt cliffs and black rocky shoreline, large dormant crater with rusty ochre inner walls in the upper center, sparse green scrub and palms in sheltered pockets, one tiny pale sand cove on lower left edge. Geologically realistic non-erupting volcano, no lava, no smoke, no glowing red light. Dramatic dark rock silhouette contrasting with tropical beach islands.

### island-waterfall.webp

Prompt completo: instrucciones comunes anteriores más el siguiente texto.

Subject: a narrow elongated emerald limestone island, tall weathered pale gray karst ridges with dense jungle crowns, irregular steep cliff coastline, a visible thin white waterfall on the lower side spilling from a small blue-green freshwater pool into a tiny sheltered jade cove, a narrow golden beach at one end. The terrain is long and angular, not round, with two uneven ridges. Photographic natural rock and trees, top down so waterfall seen as a white cascade stripe along terrain.

### island-atoll.webp

Prompt completo: instrucciones comunes anteriores más el siguiente texto.

Subject: an unmistakable natural tropical coral atoll, irregular almost closed ring of pale pinkish ivory sand and scattered low palms surrounding a large luminous blue-green central lagoon. A single narrow opening in the reef ring at lower right. Low flat terrain, many tiny coral rock textures, no mountain, no dense central forest. Large clean central lagoon dominates the island, and outside the very narrow shallow water fringe is transparent alpha. Asymmetric ring silhouette, no perfect geometric circle.
